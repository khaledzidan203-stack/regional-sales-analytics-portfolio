# Publication Allowlist

Only reviewed, data-free material may be published. Inclusion here does not bypass sanitization or validation.

Allowed after review:

- generic documentation and diagrams;
- generalized V12 analytical source;
- reproducibly generated offline frontend;
- sanitized Tauri and Rust source;
- architecture diagrams that contain no business data;
- public build and installation instructions;
- KPI, LFL, Recovery, Delivery and Data Quality methodology;
- XLSX export documentation;
- tests and validation scripts;
- package metadata and lockfiles;
- engine-neutral SQL references;
- Power BI mapping documentation;
- the reviewed abstract overview image at `docs/assets/Regional Sales Analytics Dashboard.png`.

Tests may use small in-memory objects or temporary files deleted during the test.

No real, synthetic, dummy or generated business dataset is allowed.

The presentation overview must contain only placeholders or illustrative shapes and must not be treated as analytical result evidence.
