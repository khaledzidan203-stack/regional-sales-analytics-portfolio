# Business Problem

A regional branch network needs a consistent decision-support view of:

- sales against total and segment budgets;
- the branches and regions contributing most to performance gaps;
- whether customer traffic, basket value, or both explain movement;
- comparable performance against the same prior-year dates;
- the direction of performance before and after an intervention;
- the contribution of a delivery component already included in Core Retail;
- opening and closure effects that can distort network comparisons;
- data exceptions that may invalidate management conclusions; and
- actionable follow-up priorities grounded in the current filter context.

The public portfolio documents these requirements without company identities, operating figures, branch identifiers, or datasets.
