# Analytical Feature Baseline

The current generalized V12 source contains:

- 18 analytical pages;
- Total Sales, Budget, Achievement, Budget Gap, Service Channel, Core Retail, Priority Sales, Customer Count and AST KPIs;
- Sales, Service Channel, Core Retail, Customer Count and AST Like-for-Like analysis;
- Strict Comparable and Total Region / Current Filters LFL scopes;
- user-selected Pre/Post Recovery and Intervention analysis;
- Expected Post scenario and Estimated Recovery;
- Delivery Channel contribution without adding it again to Total Sales;
- Data Quality review and validation rules;
- current-page and full-analysis multi-sheet XLSX export;
- searchable multi-select slicers;
- chart cross-filtering.

This baseline is validated from the committed generalized source.

It does not authorize publication of business data, analytical-result screenshots, executables or installers.
