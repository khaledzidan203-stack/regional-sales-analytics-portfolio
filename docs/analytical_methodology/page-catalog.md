# 18-page Analytical Catalog

| # | Page | Purpose | Main KPIs / visuals | Business question |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Executive Overview | Consolidated management view | Sales, Budget, Achievement, Gap, AST, mixes, trends | Where is performance now? |
| 2 | Budget Gap | Isolate target variance | Total/segment gaps, bridge, city and branch rankings | What creates the shortfall? |
| 3 | Sales Like-for-Like | Compare aligned sales periods | LFL, daily/cumulative/weekly/monthly charts, contributors | Is sales improving on a comparable basis? |
| 4 | Core Retail Like-for-Like | Isolate the base retail channel | Core Retail LFL and branch contributors | Is underlying retail improving? |
| 5 | Service Channel Like-for-Like | Isolate the service channel | Service Channel LFL, trends, branch detail | How is the service component changing? |
| 6 | Customer Count & AST Like-for-Like | Separate traffic and value | Customer LFL, weighted AST movement, quadrants | Is change driven by visits or basket value? |
| 7 | Recovery & Intervention Impact | Compare selected Pre/Post windows | Pre/Post LFL, momentum, gap compression, expected post | Did momentum change after intervention? |
| 8 | Delivery Channel Impact | Measure an embedded component | Delivery sales, penetration, uplift, gap recovery | What does Delivery contribute without double counting? |
| 9 | Service Channel Analysis | Evaluate service performance | Sales, budget, achievement, mix, city/branch charts | Which branches drive service results? |
| 10 | Core Retail Analysis | Evaluate core performance | Sales, budget, achievement, Priority rate | Where is Core Retail weak or strong? |
| 11 | Priority Sales Analysis | Review priority-product contribution | Priority Sales, rate, gap to configured target | Where is priority mix underperforming? |
| 12 | Customer Count & AST | Diagnose traffic and basket size | Customers, weighted AST, trend, branch quadrants | Which operating lever explains sales? |
| 13 | City Performance | Compare geographic groups | Sales, target, gap, achievement, drivers | Which regions require attention? |
| 14 | Branch Detail | Inspect one or all branches | Sales, segments, budget, AST, monthly trend | What explains an individual branch result? |
| 15 | Category & Size | Compare structural segments | Achievement, gap, mix by category/size | Do format characteristics affect performance? |
| 16 | New & Closed Branches | Separate lifecycle effects | Ramp-up, operating status, post-close checks | How do openings and closures affect totals? |
| 17 | Action Plan Generator | Convert findings to follow-up | Issue, evidence, scope, action, timing, impact | What should management review next? |
| 18 | Data Quality | Gate interpretation with readiness | Score, issue counts, detailed review queue | Is the model ready for decision use? |
